A Different Perspective

Maybe the Problem Was Never
Your Willpower.

Lipedema is a chronic condition affecting the fat tissue — most commonly in the legs, hips and buttocks — that is fundamentally different from ordinary weight gain. It is estimated to affect a significant proportion of women, yet it remains widely misdiagnosed and misunderstood.

The fat in lipedema behaves differently. It does not respond to standard calorie restriction the way ordinary fat does. It is often painful, tender to the touch, prone to swelling, and disproportionate to the rest of the body.

"You were not failing the diet.
The diet was failing you."

If you have been diagnosed with lipedema — or strongly suspect it — this protocol was created specifically for you.
